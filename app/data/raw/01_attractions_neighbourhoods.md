# Attractions and Neighbourhoods

## Marina Bay
Marina Bay is Singapore's modern civic and financial core. Key sights include
Marina Bay Sands (an integrated resort with a rooftop observation deck),
Gardens by the Bay (a large park with the Supertree Grove and two climate-controlled
conservatories, Flower Dome and Cloud Forest), and the Merlion Park, home of the
half-lion, half-fish national symbol. The Marina Bay waterfront promenade is popular
for evening walks, and the Spectra light-and-water show runs nightly outside Marina
Bay Sands. Most Marina Bay attractions are outdoor or semi-outdoor, though the
conservatories at Gardens by the Bay are indoor and air-conditioned.

## Civic District / Colonial Core
This area around the Padang contains many of Singapore's colonial-era buildings,
including the Victoria Theatre and Concert Hall, the Asian Civilisations Museum,
and the National Gallery Singapore, housed in the former Supreme Court and City
Hall buildings. The National Gallery and Asian Civilisations Museum are indoor
attractions well suited to hot or rainy days.

## Chinatown
Chinatown is a heritage neighbourhood suited to cultural experiences. Highlights
include the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, Sri Mariamman Temple (Singapore's oldest
Hindu temple), Thian Hock Keng Temple, and the Chinatown Street Market for souvenirs
and local snacks. Many temples and the Chinatown Heritage Centre are indoor.

## Kampong Glam and Little India
Kampong Glam is centred on the Sultan Mosque and Arab Street, known for textile and
carpet shops, cafes, and street art. Little India, centred on Serangoon Road,
features the Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple, Tekka Centre (a wet market and hawker
centre), and shophouses with vibrant facades. Both districts are good choices for
cultural and food-focused half-day visits.

## Sentosa Island
Sentosa is a resort island reachable by cable car, monorail, or on foot via a
boardwalk. It hosts Universal Studios Singapore, S.E.A. Aquarium, several beaches,
and the Skyline Luge. Sentosa suits a full outdoor day, with several indoor
options (aquarium, indoor play attractions) if weather turns wet.

## Singapore Zoo, River Wonders and Night Safari (Mandai)
The Mandai wildlife precinct in the north of Singapore includes the Singapore Zoo,
River Wonders, and the Night Safari (a nocturnal, mostly open-air experience).
These are largely outdoor attractions and better suited to dry weather.

## Orchard Road
Orchard Road is Singapore's main shopping belt, lined with malls such as ION
Orchard and Paragon. Because most of Orchard Road's attractions are inside
air-conditioned malls, it is a reliable indoor option on a rainy day.

## Indoor attractions for rainy days
Good indoor options across Singapore include: the National Gallery Singapore, the
ArtScience Museum, the Gardens by the Bay conservatories, the Singapore Science
Centre, the National Museum of Singapore, the Asian Civilisations Museum, and
shopping malls along Orchard Road.
