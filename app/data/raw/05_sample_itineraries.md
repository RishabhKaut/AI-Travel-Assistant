# Sample Itineraries

## Three-day classic itinerary (mixed indoor/outdoor)

**Day 1 - Marina Bay and Civic District**
- Morning: Merlion Park, Marina Bay waterfront walk (outdoor)
- Midday: National Gallery Singapore or Asian Civilisations Museum (indoor)
- Afternoon: Gardens by the Bay - Supertree Grove (outdoor) and Flower Dome /
  Cloud Forest conservatories (indoor)
- Evening: Spectra light show at Marina Bay Sands (outdoor), dinner at Lau Pa
  Sat hawker centre (semi-outdoor, covered)

**Day 2 - Culture: Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Glam**
- Morning: Buddha Tooth Relic Temple and Chinatown Street Market (mostly
  outdoor/covered market)
- Midday: Lunch at Maxwell Food Centre (semi-outdoor, covered)
- Afternoon: Little India - Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple, Tekka Centre (indoor
  and covered)
- Evening: Kampong Glam - Sultan Mosque, Arab Street, Haji Lane cafes (mixed)

**Day 3 - Sentosa or Mandai wildlife**
- Full day at Sentosa (beaches, S.E.A. Aquarium indoor, Universal Studios mixed
  indoor/outdoor) OR a full day at Mandai (Singapore Zoo / River Wonders,
  mostly outdoor)

## Family-friendly three-day itinerary
- Day 1: Gardens by the Bay conservatories (indoor), Singapore Science Centre
  (indoor), short Marina Bay walk (outdoor)
- Day 2: Singapore Zoo and River Wonders (outdoor)
- Day 3: Sentosa - S.E.A. Aquarium (indoor) and beaches (outdoor), Skyline Luge

## Rainy-day backup plan
Swap any outdoor half-day for: National Gallery Singapore, ArtScience Museum,
Gardens by the Bay conservatories, Singapore Science Centre, or an Orchard Road
mall, all of which are indoor/air-conditioned.
