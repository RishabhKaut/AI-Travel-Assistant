# Cultural and Practical Travel Tips

## Climate
Singapore has a tropical climate that is warm and humid year-round, with
temperatures typically between 24-32°C. Short, heavy showers can occur any
month, so it is wise to keep indoor alternatives in mind regardless of season
and to check the live forecast closer to the travel date.

## Language and connectivity
English is an official language and is widely used for signage and by most
businesses, alongside Mandarin, Malay, and Tamil. Free Wi-Fi is common in
malls and cafes; prepaid tourist SIM cards are available at the airport.

## Etiquette
Singapore is multicultural, with Chinese, Malay, Indian, and other communities.
Visitors should remove shoes before entering temples and some homes, dress
modestly at religious sites, and note that chewing gum sales are restricted
(though possession/use is not, contrary to popular myth). Littering, jaywalking,
and eating on the MRT are subject to fines.

## Currency and payments
The local currency is the Singapore Dollar (SGD). Cashless payment (cards,
PayNow, Grab) is widely accepted; small hawker stalls may prefer cash. It is
useful to convert at least a small amount of your home currency to SGD for
hawker centres and markets.

## Family travel
Singapore is considered very family-friendly: strollers are easily accommodated
on public transport, baby-care rooms are common in malls, and attractions such
as the Singapore Zoo, River Wonders, Science Centre, and Gardens by the Bay
cater well to children.

## Safety
Singapore is regarded as one of the safer destinations for travellers, with low
petty-crime rates, though normal precautions with valuables are still advised.
