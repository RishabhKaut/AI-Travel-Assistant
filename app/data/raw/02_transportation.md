# Local Transportation Guidance

## MRT and LRT (rail)
The Mass Rapid Transit (MRT) is the fastest and most convenient way to move
between major districts. It runs roughly from 5:30am to midnight. An EZ-Link
or NETS FlashPay stored-value card, or a contactless bank card, can be tapped
directly at faregates; single-trip tickets are also available at stations.

## Buses
An extensive bus network covers areas the MRT does not reach directly. The same
EZ-Link/contactless card used on the MRT works on buses. Google Maps and the
Citymapper or SBS Transit apps give reliable real-time bus routing.

## Taxis and ride-hailing
Metered taxis are widely available and can be flagged on the street or booked
via apps such as Grab and ComfortDelGro. Grab (ride-hailing) is commonly used by
visitors and supports cashless payment.

## Walking
Singapore's compact core, covered walkways, and air-conditioned underground
malls make many central areas very walkable, especially useful for shifting
between an outdoor and an indoor activity on the same day.

## Getting to Sentosa
Sentosa can be reached by the Sentosa Express monorail from VivoCity, by cable
car from Mount Faber or HarbourFront, by bus, or on foot/bicycle via the
Sentosa Boardwalk.

## Practical tips
- Download a transit app before arrival; fares are calculated by distance.
- Avoid the evening rush hour (roughly 6-8pm) on the MRT if travelling with
  luggage or children.
- Singapore drives on the left; look right first when crossing smaller roads.
